Correct AI Usage

I think AI can be helpful when it is used the right way. AI can help students understand hard topics and give them ideas when they are stuck. Students should still do their own work and make sure they understand what they are turning in. AI should be used as a tool to help you learn, not as a way to do all of your work for you.

Your reasoning gives a good explanation of how AI can be used correctly in school. You make it clear that AI can help students learn without replacing their own work. One thing you could also consider is that students should check information from AI because it may not always be correct. Overall, your reasoning shows that AI can be useful when students use it responsibly and still do their own thinking.

Incorrect AI Usage

Cheating on Assignments
Students may use AI to complete an entire assignment instead of doing their own work.

Copying AI Answers
Students may copy AI responses without checking if the information is correct.

Using AI Without Permission
Students may use AI on an assignment when their teacher does not allow it.

I think AI can be a helpful tool when it is used correctly. Students should not use AI to do all of their work for them. It is important for students to understand their own assignments. Using AI the wrong way can keep students from actually learning.
