Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

In 2017, the WannaCry ransomware attack affected computers around the world. The malware locked files on infected computers and demanded money to unlock them. The attack caused problems for many businesses and organizations. This event was important because it showed how quickly malware can spread and why cybersecurity is important.
